# MaHaHe

We are a small team of system administrators and DevOps engineers. We run
our own infrastructure on Kubernetes and share what we build along the way.

## Projects

- **[Helm charts](/helm/)**: Foundry VTT, YouTrack and Prometheus exporters
  for Proxmox VE and HAProxy, published as OCI charts.
- **[taiga-contrib-openid-auth](https://github.com/mahahe-it/taiga-contrib-openid-auth)**:
  OpenID Connect login for Taiga.

Everything else is on [GitHub](https://github.com/mahahe-it).
